Before you go live

1. Change the default admin password in Account & Security.
2. Edit your content across the sections on the left.
3. When you're happy, go to Backup / Export and download your updated content file, then upload it to your hosting so every visitor sees your changes — not just this browser. Full plain-language upload instructions are included with your project files.
